Privacy Policy

HireThem · Effective date: October 31, 2026

HireThem helps users organize recruiter contacts and send job-outreach emails from a Gmail account they connect. This policy explains what information the service processes and why.

Before publishing: Review this policy against the live app's actual data handling. Do not publish it until the statements below accurately describe your implementation. Add a working contact and a real account/data deletion process.

Information we process

  • Account information: information supplied during sign-up or login, such as your email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Resume and profile information: resume files and any profile details you choose to provide.
  • Gmail authorization: Google account identity and OAuth authorization data needed to connect your Gmail account and send messages you initiate. HireThem should request only the Gmail permissions shown on Google's consent screen.
  • Recruiter and campaign information: recruiter contact details, email drafts, send status, timestamps, and campaign history associated with your account.
  • Technical information: basic application and diagnostic information generated when you use the service.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, display recruiter contacts, store and use resumes, prepare outreach messages, send messages at your direction, enforce sending limits, prevent duplicate sends, maintain campaign history, troubleshoot errors, and protect the service.

Gmail and Google user data

When you connect Gmail, you authorize HireThem through Google's OAuth flow. HireThem uses the granted Gmail permission to perform the email-sending actions you request. Connecting Gmail does not give other HireThem users access to your mailbox. Do not connect an account unless you understand and accept the permissions shown by Google.

HireThem's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. HireThem does not use Google user data for advertising, sell it, or allow another user to access your mailbox through the service.

Storage and service providers

HireThem uses third-party infrastructure providers, including Supabase for application data and storage, and Google for account authorization and Gmail services. Information may be processed by these providers to operate the service, subject to their terms and privacy practices. Access controls are intended to keep each user's resume, Gmail authorization, and campaign history separate from other users.

Sharing

HireThem does not sell personal information. When you send an outreach email, the message and any attached resume are transmitted to the recipient you selected. Information may also be processed by infrastructure providers that support the service, or disclosed where required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to operate the service and maintain your account and campaign history. To request account or data deletion, contact mk5547655@gmail.com. A deletion request should cover your account data, uploaded resume, stored Gmail authorization tokens, and campaign history. Some information may remain where retention is required by law or necessary for security.

Security

HireThem uses access controls and server-side handling intended to protect stored information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials private and disconnect Google access if you no longer want HireThem to use your Gmail authorization.

Your choices

You can choose whether to upload a resume or connect Gmail. You can revoke HireThem's Google access through your Google Account security settings. You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information, subject to applicable law.

Children

HireThem is intended for adults seeking employment and is not designed for children under 18.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
